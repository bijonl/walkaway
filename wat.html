If you take that next step and your concern proves valid, what happens?

Consider money, financing, timing, flexibility, operations or reputation. Include anything important that remains uncertain.

Check the decision

Is the evidence enough to justify the next step?

Adequate: enough support to defend the step.
Mixed: support exists but important gaps remain.
Inadequate: relies mainly on assumptions or missing proof.

Do you understand what happens if you are wrong?

Judge how clear the consequence is, not how likely it is.

How easily could you undo the next step?

High: limited loss or constraint.
Moderate: meaningful cost or delay.
Low: difficult to recover money, rights, timing or flexibility.

How much are prior effort, money spent or deadline pressure pushing you forward?

Low: little influence.
Present: some influence.
Material: a significant reason for continuing.


Past effort does not create future merit.
